How is it run? *

Guests see both of these on the invite, in the waiting room, and in their recap.

Paste the Zoom or Meet link. Guests see it when they join. Turn captions on in that call before you open the host console. Vintage Fork doesn't carry the audio or video itself — your call does.

Up to 24 guests for the pilot. Once this many have joined, the invite stops admitting more. Your own console and monitoring screens don't count.

Who's running it

Runs the tasting and holds control.

Must be someone other than the host. Only this person can force takeover from 15 to 45 seconds if the host's device fails. Authorised now, before the evening — it can't be arranged mid-tasting. You can save a draft without one. You can't launch without one. Your backup host can see and edit everything about this tasting.

What happens next

Saving creates a draft. Nothing is shared with guests until you generate an invite.

Then you'll:

  1. Build the flight
  2. Add trivia for every tea
  3. Generate the invite
  4. Launch on the night
